Research discipline

A transparent path from idea to evidence

  1. State the hypothesis. Define the mechanism and what evidence could contradict it.
  2. Document the data. Record origin, time range, cleaning choices and known limitations.
  3. Separate development from evaluation. Protect out-of-sample evidence from repeated tuning.
  4. Model implementation constraints. Include costs, liquidity, timing and operational assumptions.
  5. Monitor risk and stability. Treat changing behaviour as a research finding, not something to hide.
  6. Publish context with results. Distinguish simulation, paper trading and live execution.

Scope: This website presents research and educational material. It does not provide personalized financial advice or promise investment performance.
